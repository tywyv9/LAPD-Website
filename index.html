SOP-101: Standard Pursuit Protocol
Field Operations

1. Initiation: Officers may initiate a vehicle pursuit when a suspect fails to yield and presents a clear risk to public safety or is suspected of a felony offense.

2. Unit Limits: Maximum of 3 primary/secondary patrol units permitted in active pursuit unless authorized by a Supervisory Rank.

3. Termination: Pursuit must be immediately terminated if weather, traffic density, or public hazards exceed reasonable safety margins.
